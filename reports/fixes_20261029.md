## 4. Findings made while fixing

1. **Optimality is hard to prove even on small instances** [OBSERVED]. With n = 15
   patients and 10 slots, 7 of the 14 DOE ILPs stopped at a 30 s limit (complete-cost
   gap 8 to 12 %) in a development run, and 11 of 14 at the 10 s limit of the smoke
   run. At production size
   (60 patients, 40 slots, 1,800 s) a proven optimum is not guaranteed [TO VERIFY];
   every result now carries its status and gap.
2. **Symmetry breaking slows HiGHS down** [OBSERVED]: on instances with n = 10, the
   cuts keep the optimum but raise the solve time from about 2 s to 40 to 60 s (one
   instance reached the 60 s limit). The option is kept, off by default [INFERRED:
   the cuts interfere with HiGHS' own symmetry detection].
3. **Warm start works** [OBSERVED]: with 0.2 s on n = 60, a cold start finds no
   incumbent while a start from B3 returns the greedy cost.
4. **The independence error shrinks once s_A fits the long slot** [OBSERVED, smoke
   scale, 2 seeds]: simulated minus model cost of the central cell is -3 %, against
   +33 % in the audited campaign where s_A = 64 > 60. Production confirmation
   [TO VERIFY].
5. **The threshold of double-booking is individual** [OBSERVED,
   `tests/test_threshold.py`]: on one slot the switch happens at c_W/c_I = 1/q_i of
   the patient served first (1.111 for q = 0.9), not at 1/q_bar.

**Conflict raised by V9** [OBSERVED]: the Z* = 70.82 of the manuscript's Table 1 was
computed with c_O = 0.5, i.e. with overtime. Without overtime the same example gives
Z* = 65.62 (same schedule up to the swap of the two 30-minute slots; E[OT] = 5.20
removed). Notebook 01 now asserts 70.82 under the published costs (regression check)
and 65.62 under C_O_BASE = 0; **the manuscript's Table 1 must report 65.62**.

## 5. Deviations from the prompt

1. **Thresholds read on calib, not on valid** (1.1, 1.5). The final models are
   refitted on fit + valid (1.3), so valid is in-sample for them; thresholds and the
   choice of the calibration method use the second half of calib, which no model
   has seen. Valid still chooses hyperparameters and boosting iterations.
2. **1.3, 1.4 and P5 share one commit** (`117ac6d`): the search, the imbalance
   mechanism and the metrics were rewritten together.
3. **K = 2 is imposed** rather than chosen by the maximal silhouette: the ILP has two
   duration classes. The cost and silhouette for K = 2 to 5 are still reported.
4. **SMOTE is skipped for the group task**: its minority share (about 35 %) already
   exceeds the ratio 0.1, and imbalanced-learn refuses a lower target ratio; only the
   balanced weights apply there.
5. **Holdout q pool subsampled to 100,000 values** for the transport to the campaign
   workers (empirical q variant), drawn once with `RANDOM_STATE`.
6. **Smoke time limit 10 s per ILP** (prompt: smoke S = 10, R = 2; no limit stated)
   to fit the 30-minute budget. Production keeps 1,800 s and a gap target of 1e-4.
7. **Q_bar / pi_A grid and idle-wait front use the first 10 seeds** (as the former
   notebook did with 5); the DOE, benchmarks, sensitivity and threshold study use
   all R seeds.
8. **`RESULTS.md` moved to `results/RESULTS.md`** (generated output, not versioned).
